Design notes

Brand direction · Raycast meets Pi

Canvas. Near-black backgrounds for the live mode (intimacy, focus), warm off-white app shell for the brand site. One saturated accent color: amber, reserved for the "speaking" state and primary CTAs. No emoji. Generous whitespace.

Typography. Inter at all sizes. Hero suggestion text is 28pt, semibold. Body is 14pt. Tiny labels are 10pt uppercase tracked 0.08em. Live mode uses high contrast (white on near-black) so glanceable in 0.4 seconds.

The translucent overlay. Inspired by Apple Live Captions and Cluely. Backdrop-filter blur, low opacity, draggable, content-protection enabled (uncapturable by screen recording when in V2 desktop mode).

The accent moment. The single most important UI element is the "Whispering in your ear" pill — that's the one frame people screenshot. It uses the amber background, black text, with a slight pulse. Earned, not constant.

Privacy as design. Every screen shows the privacy state. "No audio recorded" appears in the live mode as a tiny footer. The settings screen makes "Delete all memory" prominent and red. Trust is the brand.
