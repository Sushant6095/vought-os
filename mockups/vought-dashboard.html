Design notes

Brand direction · Linear meets Cresta

Light SaaS aesthetic, dark when it matters. Dashboard is clean white-on-white, dense but breathable. The live call view flips to near-black to match the urgency and intimacy of the rep's actual moment — that's the contrast that sells.

Color system. Indigo as primary brand color (signals enterprise SaaS). Amber accent reserved for live AI suggestions — same accent as Cyrano, signaling the shared engine. Emerald for positive metrics, rose for concerning ones. No more than four colors visible at once on any screen.

Density. Vought is for managers who live in dashboards. We err on the side of more numbers, more rows, more density. Dense ≠ ugly. Tables not cards for the team performance grid.

The "Now · Vought suggestion" card. The hero element of the live call screen. Amber background, large suggestion text, source attribution to the playbook below. Same visual language as Cyrano's whisper card — that's the moment that ties the two products together for investors.

AI Analyst. A natural-language box at the bottom of the dashboard ("Ask anything about your calls"). Cresta-killer feature, easy to demo, sells the platform.
