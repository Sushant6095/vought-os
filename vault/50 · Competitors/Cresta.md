# Cresta

> The leader in enterprise real-time agent assist. Our closest analog.

## Numbers

- **Valuation:** $1.6B (Series D, late 2024)
- **ARR:** ~$52M (2025 Sacra estimate)
- **Funded:** $270M total
- **Founded:** 2017 (Tim Shi, Zayd Enam, Sebastian Thrun)
- **HQ:** San Francisco

## Product surface

Three pillars (mirrors what we plan):
1. **AI Agent** (autonomous AI for inbound calls) — Vought Receptionist's analog
2. **Agent Assist** (real-time AI helping human reps during calls) — Vought Copilot's analog
3. **Conversation Intelligence** (post-call analytics, coaching, QA) — Vought Insights' analog

Plus: AI Receptionist for SMBs, AI Analyst (natural language queries over call data), Real-Time Translation, Knowledge Agent.

## Their wedge

Real-time whisper coaching for call-center agents. Sub-200ms whisper latency claimed. Used by Verizon, Holiday Inn, Vivint, EarthLink.

## Their gap (= our opening)

**Form factor.** Desktop overlay only. Reps glued to a second monitor reading text suggestions. Their best-in-class latency doesn't matter if the rep can't read fast enough.

**Voice.** No voice cloning. Their TTS (when used) is generic.

**Use case.** Enterprise contact center only. No consumer product. No SMB Receptionist that ships fast.

## How we win

1. **In-ear, not on-screen.** Reps walk, talk, look at the customer.
2. **User's own cloned voice.** No parrot effect.
3. **Consumer brand flywheel.** Vought Personal drives awareness; Vought for Teams converts.

## Risk to us

- Cresta has 3 years of contact-center playbook data. Their RAG quality is better than ours initially.
- Their sales motion is mature. Six-figure deals close quickly.
- They could bundle ElevenLabs and ship in-ear themselves in 12-18 months.

## Strategic recommendation

Beat them in the AirPods form factor before they catch up. The 18-month window is real.
