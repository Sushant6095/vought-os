# Observe.ai

> Our visual design reference benchmark. Not our most direct product competitor — more focused on contact center workflow than real-time whisper.

## Numbers

- **Funded:** $214M (Series C from SoftBank Vision Fund 2, 2022)
- **Valuation:** ~$1.4B
- **HQ:** San Francisco

## Product surface

- Conversation intelligence (post-call analytics, scoring)
- Real-time agent assist (similar to Cresta but smaller install base)
- Workforce management
- Quality management automation

## Their wedge

Workflow integration with existing contact center stacks. They embed deeply into Genesys, Cisco, etc. The product is sticky because it's woven into the QA workflow.

## What we learn from them (design)

- Dark canvas storytelling with subtle blue gradients (we replace blue with amber)
- Sticky long-form section transitions with parallax layering of UI screenshots
- Big metric numbers counting up on scroll
- Platform page that earns engineering trust through diagrams

## How we differ visually

- Observe is enterprise-soft (friendly, illustration-heavy)
- We are enterprise-serious (operating equipment, mono numerics, no illustrations)
