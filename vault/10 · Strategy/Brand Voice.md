# Brand Voice

> The voice every piece of copy must match. Read before writing any string the user will see.

## Tagline

*Intelligence for live conversations.*

No tagline below it. The brand stands on the positioning alone.

## Voice traits

**Confident.** Declarative sentences. No hedging. "Vought listens to your live conversations" — never "Vought can help you with…"

**Brief.** Headlines are 3-7 words. Body sentences are 12-22 words. Short.

**Specific.** "412ms whisper latency" beats "low latency." "The line your top closer would have said" beats "intelligent suggestions."

**Dry.** Occasional understatement. Never cute. Never glib.

**Quiet.** No exclamation marks. No bold-italics. No emoji.

## Forbidden phrases

- "AI-powered"
- "Supercharge" / "supercharged"
- "Revolutionize"
- "10x" or "100x"
- "Game-changer"
- "Crush it"
- "Unlock"
- "Unlock the power of"
- "Beautifully designed"
- "Loved by [audience] everywhere"
- "Limited time"
- "Hurry"
- Anything that sounds like a 2015 startup pitch deck

## Forbidden tones

- Hype tone (Slack-blog energy)
- Self-celebrating tone ("we're so excited")
- Mystery tone ("something big is coming")
- Performatively casual tone ("hey friends")
- Performatively formal tone ("we are pleased to announce")

## Preferred constructions

- *X does Y.* — full sentence, period.
- *X. Y.* — two short sentences for rhythm.
- *Numbers in mono.* — 412ms. 73%. $2.3B.
- *Named specifics.* — "Cresta" rather than "incumbents."

## Examples

✗ "Supercharge your sales calls with AI-powered insights that help you close more deals."

✓ "Vought whispers the line your top closer would have said. In your own voice. In your ear."

✗ "Loved by sales teams everywhere, our platform revolutionizes how revenue teams operate."

✓ "TripleByte closed $1.4M more last quarter. Suggestion acceptance tripled vs their prior tool."

✗ "Beautifully designed and thoughtfully crafted."

✓ "The voice that closes the deal."

## When in doubt

Ask: *would a principal designer at Apple write this on their landing page?* If no, rewrite.
