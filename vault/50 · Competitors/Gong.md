# Gong

> Conversation intelligence giant. Mostly post-call, not real-time. Adjacent rather than direct.

## Numbers

- **Valuation:** $4.5B (secondary, 2025)
- **ARR:** ~$500M run rate (2025)
- **Funded:** $584M total
- **Founded:** 2015
- **HQ:** San Francisco
- **Customers:** 4,000+ including Twilio, Hubspot, Slack, Pinterest

## Product surface

- Call recording + transcription
- Deal intelligence (pipeline visibility)
- Forecasting
- Coaching (recorded call review)
- Engagement (email tracking)

## Their wedge

The default conversation intelligence tool for SaaS sales orgs >50 reps. Massive integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft).

## What they don't do

- **Real-time whisper coaching.** All post-call. By the time their analytics surface a missed opportunity, the deal moment has passed.
- **Voice cloning.** Not in their product.
- **Inbound phone AI.** Not their market.

## How we win where we overlap

We don't try to win post-call analytics — Gong owns it. We win the *live moment*. If a rep follows a Vought suggestion mid-call, they don't need Gong to tell them what they missed post-call.

## Risk to us

- They could ship real-time. They have the data, the relationships, and the cash.
- They could acquire ElevenLabs (unlikely but possible).
- Their CFO-budget capture is real — sales orgs already paying $50K/yr for Gong may not add a second tool.

## Strategic recommendation

Position as "Gong for the live moment, not the recap." Don't try to displace Gong; sit alongside it. Many of our buyers already love Gong.
